AstralWake 0.2.5 — Robot integration DEVELOPMENT TEST, September 30, 2026

Run AstralWake-Shipping.exe with its AstralWake and Engine folders beside it.
Unreal Editor and the Unreal SDK are not required. Runtime DLLs are included.
This is a compiled Shipping candidate, NOT a final beta or approved public release.

ARC-9: patrol enemies on all 12 current base-game planets. New surface regions
populate as you explore. Directional optical detection requires exposure and
line of sight. Cover and moving behind the optic break detection.
Q-01 Queen: one elite spawn on Nyxara and Vespera Prime only. Eight articulated
legs, terrain foot placement, directional sensors and telegraphed attacks.
AR-R3: F12 while piloting a damaged small ship near the available AW-01 carrier
opens the repair confirmation. Enter confirms 6,000 LOCAL credits. Esc cancels.
The drone launches, tracks your ship, scans, welds, rebuilds mesh layers and
returns to the current carrier position. Failed dispatches refund the payment.
Online credits are separate. This service currently runs in local exploration.

V changes camera, J opens destinations, Shift runs on foot.
F10 visits the station; F6 boards the Carrier Flight Deck when available.

Known development limits: visuals still need final reference-quality art polish;
several authored clips share procedural motion; networked robot AI and remote
repair are unfinished. Reconstruction is an ordered triangle-layer effect, with
existing broad ship collision retained, rather than fully destructible physics.
Carrier bay docking uses a service anchor and needs clearance testing on AW-01.
No clean Windows VM or final performance certification has been completed.

The Last Belt DLC is excluded. The older online simulation server still needs
compatibility work; a running identity service does not validate online battles.
Keep the September 27 final beta separately. This test does not replace it.
