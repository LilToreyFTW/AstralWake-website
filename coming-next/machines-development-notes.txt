AstralWake planetary machines development test - September 30, 2026

Extract all files and run AstralWake-Shipping.exe. Keep the AstralWake and Engine folders beside it. Unreal Editor is not required.

Fly-Robot, Rollingweaver, Phycho, Surveyor, CautionBot and Annihilator populate local campaign landing regions on all twelve planets. Explore roughly 80-250 metres beyond the initial landing pad. Annihilator spawns with exactly two separate Spotter drones; CautionBot has one supporting air drone. FullTilt has exactly three legs and appears only on Solace Hollow and Astraea's Rest. Hatriarch is a high-threat event encounter on Kryll or Eclipsera: the configurable default threshold is twelve defeated machines across exploration regions; it never appears as a normal roaming spawn.

Player rifles, rail weapons, purchase-terminal weapons and ship projectiles can damage the machines. Surveyor scans and relays sightings without attacking. Fly-Robot can trace and cling to blocking walls or ceilings, align its feet, observe, anticipate and leap. Rollingweaver uses physics-driven spherical contact, warns for two seconds, then detonates; its optic can disable it. Phycho closes in and uses bounded flame damage with cooldown. CautionBot spins up before suppression. Annihilator requires recent Spotter data, aims before firing, and gives a three-second visible incoming mortar arc and ground warning. FullTilt warns before radial damage and character knockback. Hatriarch sheds outer armor and becomes more aggressive as health falls.

The prior AW-SC1 companion, inventory and robot work remain included. Companion & Salvage is on the home menu; F6 opens the carrier Flight Deck, then E at the rear right companion dock.
