Four September 30 development builds explore planetary machines, combat robots, the AW-SC1 companion, and scavenging. These are previews of work in progress—not approved public releases. Their features remain local to campaign play unless noted.

AstralWake-Machines-Development-Test-20260930-Windows

PLANETARY MACHINES

Machine encounters spread through landing regions and newly explored sectors on all twelve worlds. Meet Fly-Robot, Rollingweaver, Phycho, Surveyor, CautionBot, Annihilator, FullTilt, and the event-only Hatriarch—with Spotter escort drones.

  • World-specific and high-threat encounter rules
  • Scans, artillery warnings, mines, suppression, flame, and armor phases
  • Local campaign combat; networked machine play is unfinished
Read the machine test notes ↗
AstralWake-Robots-Development-Test-20260930-Windows

ROBOT INTEGRATION

ARC-9 patrols the twelve worlds. Q-01 Queen appears as a restricted elite on Nyxara and Vespera Prime. The AR-R3 can be dispatched from a nearby AW-01 to repair and reconstruct a damaged ship.

  • Spot optical detection, line of sight, and cover
  • Request a local repair for 6,000 local credits; failed dispatches refund
  • Network-authoritative robot AI and remote repairs remain unfinished
Read the robot test notes ↗
AstralWake-SC1-Development-Test-20260930-Windows

AW-SC1 COMPANION

Check on SC1 from the home-menu service bay or the carrier’s rear starboard Flight Deck. Feed it from your existing inventory and track its level, energy, maintenance, upgrades, and stored salvage.

  • Five feeding items and persistent local companion progress
  • Timed salvage that you collect manually; basket capacity is 32
  • Autonomous crate searching and multiplayer companion progress are unfinished
Read the companion test notes ↗
AstralWake-Scavenging-Development-Test-20260930-Windows

PLANETARY SCAVENGING

Search field cases, cargo boxes, and trash containers near ports and authored surface sites. Collect supplies, feed SC1, refill compatible ammunition, or sell an item for local credits.

  • Explore planet-specific food and maintenance-item drops
  • 57 authored icons for food, containers, ammunition, attachments, and parts
  • Attachment equipping, crafting, and networked scavenging are unfinished
Read the scavenging test notes ↗
